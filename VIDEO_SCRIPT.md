# CookWithMe AR — Demo Video Script (target 2:45, hard cap 3:00)

Record the Lens Studio preview (screen recording, e.g. OBS or Windows
Game Bar Win+G). Speak in English (the judges are international) — clear,
slow, one sentence per shot. No keys or credentials ever appear on
screen (there are none in this project).

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

Say: "Cooking with a phone is miserable. The screen locks, your hands
are wet or covered in flour, and touching the phone mid-recipe is
unhygienic. CookWithMe AR puts the recipe where the cooking happens —
beside the workspace, over the stove — with nothing to touch."

Show: the Lens Studio preview window, empty workspace view.

## 0:20–0:50 — The recipe card appears

Say: "I open the Lens and the recipe card appears beside the workspace
— not over it. Step 1 of 6: boil water. And at the bottom, only what I
need right now: water."

Show: card appearing, Step 1, ingredient line "You need now: Water".

## 0:50–1:30 — Steps + timer over the stove

Say: "Each step shows one instruction and its ingredients. On timed
steps, the timer starts by itself and sits over the stove, where the
pan is — so I can watch it while stirring."

Show: move to Step 2 (ginger, cardamom) and Step 3 (tea leaves); timer
counting down over the stove area.

## 1:30–2:10 — The hands-free moment (most important shot)

Say: "Here is the part that matters. When the timer finishes, the Lens
moves to the next step by itself. No tap, no touch — my hands stay on
the cooking."

Show: timer reaching zero and the step auto-advancing. Then click the
Next and Back zones once each to show manual control.

## 2:10–2:40 — Finish + restart

Say: "After the last step, a Done panel appears. One tap restarts the
whole recipe."

Show: stepping to the end, Done panel, tap to restart at Step 1.

## 2:40–2:55 — Close

Say: "CookWithMe AR — a hands-free kitchen assistant for SPECS glasses,
built in Lens Studio with CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP. Repo link is in
the description."

Show: the repo README on screen for 2 seconds (optional), then end.

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Preview window maximised, 1080p, mouse cursor visible on clicks
- [ ] Total length under 3:00 (aim 2:45)
- [ ] No terminal, no keys, no passwords anywhere in any frame
- [ ] Upload to YouTube as Public, put the repo link in the description
